Prices by product type: Frankfurt, Barcelona and Hamburg desks

Official Price Index figures from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index breaks each city into product types when enough list prices stand behind the figure. This note compares those official type-level medians in Frankfurt and Barcelona — flex desk, fixed desk and private office on a per-desk basis — and the two desk products in Hamburg. Hamburg private-office figures are omitted here. The index was generated at 11:17 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026. The catalogue underneath is wider than the priced sample: 23 spaces and 11 operators in Frankfurt, 48 spaces and 15 operators in Barcelona, and 37 spaces and 15 operators in Hamburg.

A flex desk is cheaper than a reserved desk in all three cities, but the gap is small in Frankfurt (€199 vs €299, and the fixed-desk sample is only two spaces) and much wider in Hamburg (€297 vs €499). Barcelona sits in between on the reserved desk (€300) and matches Frankfurt on flex (€199). Private offices, quoted per desk, have a median of €900 in Frankfurt and €295 in Barcelona — in Barcelona that is actually a little below the reserved-desk median, which is a quirk of the published lists, not a recommendation. Frankfurt’s priced sample is still thin, so those medians can move when a few more lists arrive. The live tables stay on the Price Index.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included.
