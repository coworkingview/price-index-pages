Private offices: Berlin, Madrid and Munich

A short data note from the CoworkingView Price Index · 24 November 2026

A private office is a closed room, still quoted per desk by the month. It is not a flex desk and it is not a meeting room. The figures below are city-level medians of the list prices that operators publish for that product.

They were read from the public Price Index API at 19:36 Europe/Berlin on 24 November 2026. Every median rests on at least five published prices. This note covers Berlin, Madrid and Munich only.

Private-office medians, euros per desk per month. n is the number of published prices; spaces and operators are the counts in that product’s sample.
City City catalogue Private office Office sample
Berlin 144 listed · 71 operators €470 n=58, 37 spaces, 21 operators
Madrid 64 listed · 21 operators €350 n=65, 26 spaces, 11 operators
Munich 33 listed · 13 operators €711 n=11, 6 spaces, 4 operators

The office sample is only part of each city’s catalogue: in Berlin it sits in 37 of 144 listed spaces, in Madrid in 26 of 64, and in Munich in 6 of 33. A building that does not publish a private-office price is not in the median. In Munich this figure matches the median already noted from the 19 November snapshot; the sample size is unchanged in the 24 November payload.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. The method is described in the methodology. Index figures are licensed CC BY 4.0: please credit CoworkingView and keep a link to the live index. They are not a valuation of any named building.
