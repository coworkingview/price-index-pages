Day-pass and meeting-room medians in six European cities

Official Price Index figures from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index is often cited for monthly desks. Two other official products sit beside those figures: the day pass, billed per day, and the meeting room, billed per hour. They are not comparable with a monthly desk, and they are not comparable with each other, because the units differ. This note reports the official type-level medians in Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Madrid and Barcelona. The index was generated at 14:23 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026. Private-office figures are omitted here.

Official Price Index medians. Day passes are euros per day; meeting rooms are euros per hour. “n” is the number of list prices; spaces and operators are the priced sample behind that product, not the whole city catalogue.

Among these six cities a day pass is cheapest in Madrid at €22, and a meeting room is also cheapest there at €27. Berlin and Barcelona share a day-pass median of €35. Munich and Frankfurt sit at the top of the meeting-room column, both at €49 an hour, and Munich has the highest day-pass median in the set, at €45. Hamburg’s day pass is €38 and its meeting room is €45. The two samples in each city are not necessarily the same buildings. The Price Index methodology keeps day passes and meeting rooms out of the overall desk median, because the units are not the same.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. A membership may include day or room credits that a walk-in booking does not.
