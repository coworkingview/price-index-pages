Coworking hubs: Berlin, Munich, Madrid and Hamburg

A catalogue count from CoworkingView · 24 November 2026

The Price Index publishes a median only when enough list prices stand behind it. The catalogue underneath is wider: it also includes spaces that are listed but do not yet have a published price in the index sample. The tables below count those live listings, read from the CoworkingView catalogue on 24 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin). They are counts of buildings, not prices. One Berlin listing marked coming soon (GESOWORX Berlin-Wedding) is left out of the totals.

Berlin is the only one of the four where the catalogue is large enough to split meaningfully by borough. Mitte alone has 66 of the 144 live listings, then Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg (29), Pankow (19) and Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf (11). Tempelhof-Schöneberg and Neukölln have 6 each, Treptow-Köpenick 4, and Spandau and Reinickendorf 1 each. One listing (Regus at Berlin Brandenburg Airport) sits outside the city boroughs and is not assigned to a Bezirk. Marzahn-Hellersdorf, Lichtenberg and Steglitz-Zehlendorf have no live listing in this snapshot.

In Hamburg the catalogue is concentrated in one borough: 29 of the 37 live listings are in Hamburg-Mitte (including HafenCity, Altstadt and Neustadt). Hamburg-Nord has 4, Altona 2, and Bergedorf and Harburg 1 each. Eimsbüttel and Wandsbek have no live listing in this snapshot.
