Operator concentration in nine European cities

Catalogue counts from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

Price medians in the CoworkingView Price Index rest on a priced sample. The catalogue underneath is wider, and it also shows how listings are spread across operators. This note counts live listings on 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin) in Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Madrid, Barcelona, Málaga, Valencia and Cologne. It is a count of listed spaces, not of priced desks, revenue or market share by floor area. Names identify operators in the catalogue; no endorsement is implied.

Live catalogue listings and operator concentration. “Largest operator” is the brand with the most listings in that city (two brands are named where they are tied). “Top 3 share” is the combined share of the three operators with the most listings.

Berlin has the broadest catalogue in the set, with 144 spaces and 62 operators. The largest operator, St. Oberholz, has 14 listings (9.7%), and the top three together hold 22.2%. Concentration is higher in smaller catalogues: in Munich the largest operator holds 36.4% of listings, and in Cologne 33.3%. In Madrid, Regus and Spaces are tied on 12 listings each (18.8%). In Barcelona the largest operator holds 18.8%, and the top three together hold half of the listings. For current desk prices, use the live index rather than these counts.
