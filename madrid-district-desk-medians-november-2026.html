Coworking desk prices by Madrid district

A Price Index note from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

Madrid’s official city figures from the CoworkingView Price Index are a useful starting point, but the districts are not interchangeable. On 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin) the live catalogue listed 64 spaces and 21 operators in and around Madrid. The medians below use the monthly flex-desk and fixed-desk list prices in that catalogue. A district median is shown only when at least three spaces in that district have a published monthly price for that desk type.

At city level the official index, generated at 11:17 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026, reads Madrid as €175 for a flex desk (n=31, 19 spaces, 10 operators) and €215 for a fixed desk (n=26, 24 spaces, 13 operators). Cite those figures for the city as a whole; the table is the district split on this day.

Tetuán has the most listings (11) and sits on the city medians: €175 flex and €215 fixed. Chamartín is higher on both (€220 and €300). Arganzuela is the least expensive district with a published pair of medians (€155 flex, €260 fixed). Chamberí and Hortaleza have a fixed-desk median (€199 and €197) but not yet three priced flex-desk spaces. Salamanca has seven listings and no published monthly desk price in this snapshot, so it appears as a count only. Four listings have no district in the catalogue, and four more sit in Alcobendas, Pozuelo de Alarcón, Rivas-Vaciamadrid and Tres Cantos, outside the municipality.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. Meeting rooms, day passes and other non-monthly rates are left out.
