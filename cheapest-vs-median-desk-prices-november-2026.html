Cheapest desk vs median in nine European cities

Official Price Index figures from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index is often cited for a single city median. The same official dataset also records the cheapest published list price and the 75th percentile. This note compares those three figures for flex desks and fixed desks in Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Madrid, Barcelona, Málaga, Valencia and Cologne. The index was generated at 15:01 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026. Figures are euros per desk per month. Hamburg private-office figures are omitted. A small official sample is still shown, with its size, rather than guessed.

The widest flex-desk gap in this set is in Berlin, where the cheapest published flex desk is €80 against a median of €209. Madrid also has a low entry point, at €85 versus a €175 median. In Frankfurt, Cologne and Valencia the cheapest flex desk sits on or next to the median, so the spread is narrow; Frankfurt and Cologne also have small official flex samples (three spaces each). In Munich the cheapest flex desk is €199 and the median is €399, with the 75th percentile on the same €399 figure. Hamburg’s cheapest flex desk is €150 against a median of €297. The two desk products in a city do not always share the same buildings.
