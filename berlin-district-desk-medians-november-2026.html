Coworking desk prices by Berlin borough

A Price Index note from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index publishes flex-desk and fixed-desk medians at city level. Berlin is large enough to split those desk prices by borough (Bezirk). The counts below are live catalogue listings on 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin): 144 spaces and 71 operators. One listing marked coming soon is already left out of that total. The medians use the monthly flex-desk and fixed-desk list prices in the same catalogue. A district median is shown only when at least three spaces in that district have a published monthly price for that desk type; thinner samples are left blank, not guessed.

At city level the official index, generated at 11:17 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026, still reads Berlin as €209 for a flex desk (n=75, 69 spaces, 39 operators) and €310 for a fixed desk (n=62, 58 spaces, 38 operators). Those are the figures to cite for the city as a whole. The table is the district split of the catalogue prices on this day.

Mitte is both the largest borough in the catalogue (66 of 144 listings) and the more expensive one for desks among the boroughs with a published median: €260 flex and €347 fixed. Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg and Pankow sit on the city flex median of €209, with a fixed desk at €299. Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf, Neukölln and Tempelhof-Schöneberg are a little below that on flex (€199). The reserved desk is above the flex desk in every borough that has both figures. Reinickendorf and Spandau each have a single listing, so no district median is published. The unassigned row is Regus at Berlin Brandenburg Airport, which sits outside the city boroughs.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. Meeting rooms, day passes and other non-monthly rates are left out.
