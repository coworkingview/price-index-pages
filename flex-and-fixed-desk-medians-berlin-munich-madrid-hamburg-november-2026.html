Flex desk or fixed desk: Berlin, Munich, Madrid and Hamburg

A short data note from the CoworkingView Price Index · 24 November 2026

A flex desk is a seat in a shared room that is not reserved for one person. A fixed desk is a reserved seat, still in a shared room, billed per desk by the month. They are not the same product, and a private office is a third product again. The table compares only the two shared-desk types.

The figures were read from the public Price Index API at 19:36 Europe/Berlin on 24 November 2026. Every median below rests on at least five published list prices. Smaller samples are omitted, not guessed. The unit is euros per desk per month.

Published medians. n is the number of published prices; spaces and operators are the counts in that product’s sample, which can be smaller than the city catalogue.

In all four cities the reserved desk sits above the flex desk, but the gap is not the same size. In Madrid the two medians are €40 apart; in Hamburg they are €202 apart. That is a difference in the published lists, not a recommendation to buy one product or the other. The two samples in a city are not necessarily the same buildings.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. The Price Index methodology keeps meeting rooms, day passes and other hourly rates out of these desk medians, because the units are not the same.
