Coworking Price Index

An open dataset maintained by CoworkingView

CoworkingView is a site for comparing coworking spaces, private offices and managed floors. Searching the catalogue is free and does not require an account. Brokerage is free of charge for tenants. The company is a licensed real estate broker in Germany (§ 34c GewO), based in Berlin.

The Price Index is the public part of that work. It collects the prices that operators publish themselves and turns them into city-level figures for flex desks, dedicated desks and private offices. A figure is only published when enough price points stand behind it; cities or types that fall short are left out, not guessed. The licence for the index and the catalogue data in the open files is CC BY 4.0.

As of 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin), the live catalogue still covered 280+ cities, including Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, London, Madrid and Dubai. On that day the catalogue listed 144 spaces in Berlin, 33 in Munich, 64 in Madrid, 37 in Hamburg, 23 in Frankfurt and 48 in Barcelona.
