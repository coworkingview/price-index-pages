Meeting rooms and private offices: Munich, Hamburg and Málaga

A short data note from the CoworkingView Price Index · November 2026

A coworking membership is often judged by the desk. Two other published prices are easy to overlook: the meeting room, usually billed by the hour, and the private office, billed per desk by the month. They are not the same product as a flex desk, and they are not interchangeable with each other, because the units differ. Do not divide one median by the other.

The figures below were read from the public Price Index API on 23 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin). Munich and Hamburg still carried a snapshot dated 19 November 2026, 10:52 UTC; Málaga had been refreshed that evening, at 21:21 Europe/Berlin. Every median rests on at least five published prices. Smaller samples are omitted, not guessed.

The two samples in each city are not necessarily the same buildings. In Munich the office figure sits in 6 of the 12 spaces, and the room figure in 7. The Price Index methodology also keeps meeting rooms, day passes and other hourly rates out of the overall desk median, because the units are not the same. A membership may include room credits that a walk-in booking does not. A private office is a closed room, still quoted per desk, not a seat in a shared room.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. Index figures are licensed CC BY 4.0: please credit CoworkingView and keep a link to the live index. They are not a valuation of any named building.
