Coworking desk prices by district in Frankfurt and Hamburg

A Price Index note from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index publishes flex-desk and fixed-desk medians at city level. This note splits the live catalogue in Frankfurt and Hamburg by district. The counts are listings on 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin): 23 spaces and 11 operators in Frankfurt, and 37 spaces and 15 operators in Hamburg. None is marked coming soon. A district median is shown only when at least three spaces in that district have a published monthly price for that desk type; thinner samples are left blank, not guessed. Hamburg private-office figures are omitted.

At city level the official index, generated at 15:01 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026, reads Frankfurt as €199 for a flex desk (n=3, 3 spaces, 2 operators) and €299 for a fixed desk (n=2, 2 spaces, 1 operators). Hamburg is €297 for a flex desk (n=10, 10 spaces, 6 operators) and €499 for a fixed desk (n=8, 8 spaces, 5 operators). Those are the figures to cite for each city as a whole. The priced sample is smaller than the catalogue, especially in Frankfurt.

In Hamburg, Neustadt (Hamburg-Mitte) is the only district with a published desk median on this day: €295 for a flex desk (n=3, 3 spaces). That sits just under the official city flex median of €297. HafenCity and Altstadt each have more than three listings, but fewer than three priced desk spaces, so no district median is published there. No Hamburg district reaches the three-space threshold for a fixed desk. These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included.
