Coworking desk prices by Barcelona district

A Price Index note from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index publishes flex-desk and fixed-desk medians at city level. Barcelona is large enough to split those desk prices by district. The counts below are live catalogue listings on 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin): 48 spaces and 15 operators. None is marked coming soon. The medians use the monthly flex-desk and fixed-desk list prices in the same catalogue. A district median is shown only when at least three spaces in that district have a published monthly price for that desk type; thinner samples are left blank, not guessed.

At city level the official index, generated at 14:23 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026, still reads Barcelona as €199 for a flex desk (n=21, 20 spaces, 6 operators) and €300 for a fixed desk (n=20, 20 spaces, 7 operators). Those are the figures to cite for the city as a whole. The table is the district split of the catalogue prices on this day.

Eixample is the largest district in the catalogue (15 of 48 listings) and has published medians of €220 for a flex desk and €300 for a fixed desk, in line with the official city fixed-desk figure. Gràcia (5 listings) is the other district with a published sample: €179.50 flex and €253.88 fixed. Sant Martí, Sarrià-Sant Gervasi, Sants-Montjuïc and Ciutat Vella each have four or more listings but fewer than three priced spaces for those desk types, so no district median is published. Two catalogue rows have no published city district. One listing is in Esplugues de Llobregat and another is at Vallsolana Garden Business Park; both sit outside the city districts.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. Meeting rooms, day passes and other non-monthly rates are left out.
