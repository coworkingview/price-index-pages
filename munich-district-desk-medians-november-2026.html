Coworking desk prices by Munich borough

A Price Index note from CoworkingView · 25 November 2026

The CoworkingView Price Index publishes flex-desk and fixed-desk medians at city level. Munich’s listings also split by borough (Bezirk). The counts below are live catalogue listings on 25 November 2026 (Europe/Berlin): 33 spaces and 13 operators. None is marked coming soon. The medians use the monthly flex-desk and fixed-desk list prices in the same catalogue. A district median is shown only when at least three spaces in that district have a published monthly price for that desk type; thinner samples are left blank, not guessed.

At city level the official index, generated at 14:23 Europe/Berlin on 25 November 2026, still reads Munich as €399 for a flex desk (n=11, 11 spaces, 6 operators) and €499 for a fixed desk (n=8, 8 spaces, 4 operators). Those are the figures to cite for the city as a whole. The table is the district split of the catalogue prices on this day.

Altstadt-Lehel holds 12 of the 33 listings and is the only borough with a published desk median on this day: €299.50 for a flex desk and €549 for a fixed desk. That flex figure sits below the official city median of €399, and the reserved desk sits above the flex desk, as in the city figures. Maxvorstadt (4 listings) and Laim (3) do not yet have three priced spaces for either desk type, so no district median is published. The remaining boroughs each have one or two listings. Two catalogue rows have no published city district. One further listing is Regus at Munich Airport, which sits outside the city boroughs.

These are list prices that operators publish, not transaction prices, and VAT may or may not be included. Meeting rooms, day passes and other non-monthly rates are left out.
